## 全局安全规则

- 所有 fan 写方法返回值仍按 `void/no acknowledgement` 处理。
- readback 名称不能证明 commanded duty；本矩阵没有任何硬件确认。
- 单调融合只能保持或提高原版 HC 冷却请求，不得降低。
- 未声明 fan capability 或无法解析的类必须阻断，不得进入 generic 控制。
- suspend/resume 和 Close policy 仅完成源码分类，运行顺序留给后续批次验证。

## 限制

- The matrix is static SRC evidence and does not execute HC assemblies or hardware backends.
- Factory identity conditions do not prove unknown firmware revisions behave identically.
- No readback is promoted to confirmed commanded duty and no void write is treated as acknowledgement.
- Outer close policies are source classifications for later lifecycle validation, not executed recovery proof.
