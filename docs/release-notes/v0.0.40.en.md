# YMCC v0.0.40

## Highlights: Xbox full screen and in-game YMCC access

- **Xbox full screen experience on Windows.** Choose Xbox full screen at startup, with Steam Big Picture as an option.
- **Open YMCC in supported fullscreen games.** Use your usual shortcut to check monitoring or adjust performance and common settings without leaving the game. Exclusive fullscreen compatibility varies with the game, device, and driver.

This version brings together mainline UI, input, fan, and device updates and improves recovery from legacy settings during upgrades.

- Settings recovery handles deeply nested legacy extensions and unrelated legacy schema fields. Incompatible settings near the size limit can fall back to the new safe defaults instead of blocking startup.
- A failed diagnostic backup does not prevent recovery. Untrusted settings cannot re-enable automatic hardware actions or startup takeover. Package integrity checks, the game-state download pause, and the background download switch remain in effect.
- This release also includes mainline updates to controller input, fan and lighting modules, Steam, Decky, and the UI.

This is a fast release. The full fixture suite was not run, and hardware support varies by model and local environment.
